# People Design

## Purpose

The people module is the core of the Shajarat Afrin project.

Every human being is represented by one permanent record that never changes its identity.

The person's information may evolve over time, but the record itself always remains the same.

---

## Permanent Identifier

Every person receives a permanent identifier.

Example:

AFR-000001

This identifier is never reused and never changed.

---

## Core Principles

- One permanent record for every person.
- No permanent deletion.
- Every change is traceable.
- Every important fact can have one or more sources.
- Privacy is respected for living people.
- Historical information is preserved..
